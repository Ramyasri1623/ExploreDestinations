Jaipur, Rajasthan

Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is famous for its stunning palaces and vibrant culture.

Goa

Kerala

ATTRACTIONS

--worth a thousand stories--

Varanasi

Varanasi, the spiritual capital of India, is famous for its ghats along the River Ganga and its vibrant religious culture.

Mysore

Known for its rich cultural heritage, Mysore is home to the magnificent Mysore Palace and the grand Dasara Festival.

Ladakh

Ladakh is a high-altitude desert with stunning landscapes, rugged mountains, and serene Buddhist monasteries.

Historical Attractions

Explore the rich heritage and history of India!

Taj Mahal

An iconic symbol of love, the Taj Mahal in Agra is a magnificent white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan.

Red Fort

Built in 1648 by Shah Jahan, the Red Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and an iconic symbol of India's rich history.

Hampi

Hampi, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, was once the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire and is known for its stunning ruins.

Konark Sun Temple

The Konark Sun Temple is a 13th-century architectural marvel dedicated to the Sun God, known for its intricate stone carvings.

Ajanta Caves

The Ajanta Caves are ancient rock-cut caves that house exquisite Buddhist sculptures and murals dating back to 2nd century BCE.

Khajuraho Temples

Famous for their erotic sculptures, the Khajuraho Temples are a UNESCO World Heritage Site built during the Chandela dynasty.

Amer Fort

Located in Jaipur, Amer Fort is known for its artistic Hindu architecture and was once the residence of the Rajput Maharajas.

Sanchi Stupa

The Great Stupa at Sanchi is one of the oldest stone structures in India, built by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE.

Golconda Fort

Once a thriving diamond trade center, Golconda Fort is a massive fortification known for its acoustics and palatial ruins.

Qutub Minar

The Qutub Minar, the tallest brick minaret in the world, is a fine example of Indo-Islamic architecture built in 1193.

Hill Stations

Discover India's most serene and scenic hill stations, perfect for a peaceful retreat!

Manali

Manali offers stunning landscapes, adventure sports, and serene retreats in the Himalayas.

Ooty

Known for its tea plantations and misty hills, Ooty is the perfect nature escape.

Darjeeling

Enjoy stunning views of the Kanchenjunga and world-famous Darjeeling tea.

Munnar

Munnar’s rolling hills and tea estates offer a tranquil getaway in the Western Ghats.

Shimla

Once a British summer capital, Shimla is known for its colonial architecture and cool climate.

Nainital

Nainital’s scenic lakes and charming town offer a perfect blend of nature and serenity.

Kodaikanal

Known as the 'Princess of Hill Stations,' Kodaikanal is famous for its mist-covered hills and waterfalls.

Mussoorie

Mussoorie’s cool climate and scenic views make it a popular destination in the Garhwal region.

Gulmarg

A paradise for snow lovers, Gulmarg is renowned for skiing and picturesque landscapes.

Coorg

Coorg is known for its lush coffee plantations, misty hills, and rich cultural heritage.
